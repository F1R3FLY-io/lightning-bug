## Performance Analysis

### Target Comparison

| Benchmark | Target (ms) | Actual P95 (ms) | Delta | Status |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|-------|--------|
| diagnostics-by-uri | 5 | 7.90 | +58% | FAIL |
| symbols-by-uri | 5 | 11.30 | +126% | FAIL |
| all-diagnostics | 5 | 5.90 | +18% | FAIL |
| all-symbols | 5 | 9.80 | +96% | FAIL |
| diagnostic-transform | 10 | 0.10 | -99% | PASS |

### Key Findings

1. **Symbol queries are the primary bottleneck**:
   - `symbols-by-uri` (10.02ms mean, 11.30ms P95) is 2x over target
   - `all-symbols` (9.33ms mean, 9.80ms P95) is nearly 2x over target

2. **Diagnostic queries exceed targets but are faster**:
   - `diagnostics-by-uri` (6.75ms mean) is 35% over target
   - `all-diagnostics` (5.58ms mean) is 12% over target

3. **Simple lookups are efficient**:
   - `document-lookup` (0.47ms) - fast
   - `active-uri` (0.35ms) - fast

4. **Transformations are well within targets**:
   - `diagnostic-transform` (0.02ms) - 500x under target
   - `symbol-flatten` (1.14ms) - efficient

### Variance Analysis

- **Very Low** (σ < 0.1ms): timing-overhead, diagnostic-transform, document-lookup, active-uri, symbol-flatten
- **Low** (σ 0.1-0.3ms): all-diagnostics, all-symbols
- **Moderate** (σ 0.3-1.0ms): diagnostics-by-uri, symbols-by-uri

Low variance indicates stable, reproducible measurements suitable for optimization experiments.

## Hypotheses for Investigation

### H-001: DataScript `or-join` clauses may be inefficient
- **Rationale**: Known DataScript limitation with `or-join`
- **Benchmarks affected**: all-diagnostics, all-symbols
- **Profiling needed**: Measure query execution breakdown

### H-002: Symbol queries lack effective indexing
- **Rationale**: Symbol queries are 2x slower than diagnostic queries
- **Benchmarks affected**: symbols-by-uri, all-symbols
- **Profiling needed**: Examine DataScript schema indices

### H-003: Query result materialization overhead
- **Rationale**: Results may be over-fetched then filtered
- **Benchmarks affected**: All query benchmarks
- **Profiling needed**: Profile result set sizes vs. used data
